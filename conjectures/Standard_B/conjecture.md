**Conjecture**: (Standard conjecture B: algebraicity of inverse Lefschetz.) Let $X/k$ be smooth projective of dimension $d$ over an algebraically closed field, with a Weil cohomology satisfying hard Lefschetz and ample class $L$. For every $0\leq i\leq d$, the inverse of

$$
L^{d-i}:H^i(X)\xrightarrow{\sim}H^{2d-i}(X)(d-i)
$$

is induced by a correspondence in $\mathrm{CH}^i(X\times X)_{\mathbf Q}$.
