**Conjecture**: (Voevodsky’s smash-nilpotence conjecture.) If $X/k$ is smooth projective and $z\in\mathrm{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}$ is numerically trivial, then some integer $N\geq1$ satisfies

$$
z^{\boxtimes N}=0\quad\text{in }\mathrm{CH}^{rN}(X^N)_{\mathbf Q}.
$$

Equivalently, numerical equivalence and smash equivalence coincide with rational coefficients.
