**Conjecture**: (Standard conjecture D: homological equals numerical equivalence.) For a smooth projective $d$-fold $X$ over an algebraically closed field and a fixed Weil cohomology $H$, a rational cycle $z\in\mathrm{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}$ satisfies

$$
\operatorname{cl}_H(z)=0
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\deg(z\cdot w)=0\quad\text{for every }w\in\mathrm{CH}^{d-r}(X)_{\mathbf Q}.
$$
