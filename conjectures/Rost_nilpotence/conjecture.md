**Conjecture**: (Rost nilpotence principle.) Let $X/k$ be smooth projective of dimension $d$, and let $L/k$ be any field extension. If

$$
\alpha\in\ker\bigl(\mathrm{CH}^d(X\times X)\to\mathrm{CH}^d(X_L\times X_L)\bigr),
$$

then $\alpha^{\circ N}=0$ for some $N\geq1$. The Chow groups here have integral coefficients, and powers mean composition of correspondences.
