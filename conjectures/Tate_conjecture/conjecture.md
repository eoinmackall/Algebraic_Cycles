**Conjecture**: (Tate conjecture: cycle-class surjectivity.) Let $X/k$ be smooth projective, with $k$ finitely generated over its prime field, and let $\ell\neq\operatorname{char}(k)$. For all $r\geq0$,

$$
\mathrm{CH}^r(X)\otimes\mathbf Q_\ell
\longrightarrow
H^{2r}_{\mathrm{et}}(X_{\bar k},\mathbf Q_\ell(r))^{G_k}
$$

is surjective, where $G_k=\operatorname{Gal}(\bar k/k)$.
