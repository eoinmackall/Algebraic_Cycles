**Conjecture**: (Standard conjecture A: Lefschetz for algebraic classes.) Let $X/k$ be smooth projective of dimension $d$, with $k$ algebraically closed. Fix a Weil cohomology satisfying hard Lefschetz, and let $L$ be cup product with an ample divisor class. Write $A_H^r(X)=\operatorname{im}(\mathrm{CH}^r(X)_{\mathbf Q}\to H^{2r}(X)(r))$. For $0\leq 2r\leq d$,

$$
L^{d-2r}:A_H^r(X)\xrightarrow{\sim}A_H^{d-r}(X)
$$

is an isomorphism.
