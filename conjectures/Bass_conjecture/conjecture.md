**Conjecture**: (Bass finite-generation conjecture.) For every regular scheme $X$ of finite type over $\operatorname{Spec}\mathbf Z$, the abelian group $K_n(X)$ is finitely generated for every $n\geq0$.

An equivalent formulation uses coherent-sheaf K-theory: $G_n(X)$ is finitely generated for every scheme $X$ of finite type over $\mathbf Z$ and every $n\geq0$.

**Note**: The finite-type hypothesis is over the integers; it cannot be replaced by finite type over an arbitrary field.
