**Conjecture**: (Standard conjecture I: Hodge standard conjecture.) Let $X/k$ be a smooth projective $d$-fold over an algebraically closed field, with an ample class $L$ and a Weil cohomology satisfying hard Lefschetz. Set

$$
P_H^r(X)=\ker\bigl(L^{d-2r+1}:A_H^r(X)\longrightarrow A_H^{d-r+1}(X)\bigr),
\qquad 0\leq 2r\leq d,
$$

where $A_H^r$ denotes rational algebraic classes modulo homological equivalence. The rational intersection form

$$
(a,b)\longmapsto (-1)^r\deg(a\cdot b\cdot L^{d-2r})
$$

is positive definite on $P_H^r(X)\otimes_{\mathbf Q}\mathbf R$.
