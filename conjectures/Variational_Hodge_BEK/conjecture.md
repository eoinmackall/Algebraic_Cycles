**Conjecture**: (Variational Hodge conjecture: de Rham formulation.) Let $k$ have characteristic zero, let $f:\mathcal X\to S$ be smooth projective with $S/k$ smooth, and let $s\in S$. If $\xi_s\in K_0(\mathcal X_s)_{\mathbf Q}$ has Chern character in the image of

$$
H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(\mathcal X/k)\longrightarrow H^*_{\mathrm{dR}}(\mathcal X_s/\kappa(s)),
$$

then some $\xi\in K_0(\mathcal X)_{\mathbf Q}$ satisfies
$\operatorname{ch}(\xi|_{\mathcal X_s})=\operatorname{ch}(\xi_s)$.
